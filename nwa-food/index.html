How to read this. Buzz score (0–100) is built mostly on Google rating × review volume — the most reliable signal — with bonus weight when a place also has Yelp corroboration, an Instagram presence, or is actually featured in one of 8 verified NWA food videos loaded into NotebookLM (YouTube ✓). A Press ~ badge is an unverified press mention reported by research, not a checked link. Each card shows only the signals it actually has; a greyed badge means we didn't find that one. Distances are estimated by city and are approximate — the Map button is the source of truth. Fast-food chains are listed once per brand (not per location). Food trucks move — the schedule shown is last-known, so tap Instagram for where they actually are today. Where no verified handle was found, the card says so rather than linking a guess. The handles themselves come from web research and could not be machine-checked (Instagram blocks unauthenticated profile lookups), so a link may occasionally 404 — search the truck's name on Instagram if so. Not built from a paid Places/Yelp API, so some review counts and handles are approximate or absent. Data collected June 26 & July 31, 2026 via grounded web research.
