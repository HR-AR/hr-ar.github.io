Is anything better than SNOO?

No reviewer consensus puts another bassinet above the SNOO for the 6-week to 6-month window on the thing it does (rocks harder when baby cries, keeps baby on the back). What exists:

  • Cradlewise Smart Crib, $1,999 ($1,749 express). The only one that does more: converts to a crib usable to 24 months, camera and monitor built in, no subscription. Reviewers call its motion gentler than SNOO. Overkill for a 5-month window, and at 5.5 weeks you have already used part of the bassinet phase. cradlewise.com
  • Delta Children Aero Smart Auto Glide, $499.99. New 2026 entrant, cry detection with side-to-side glide and app, no subscription. Too new for a track record; this is the one to watch for baby #3. deltachildren.com
  • Maxi-Cosi Starling, $665 (down from $950). Cry-type detection and five motions, but a 20 lb cap and no height adjust. maxicosi.com
  • HALO BassiNest Swivel Sleeper 3.0, $199–$289. The standard non-motorized answer: swivel, drop wall, vibration and sounds on the Premiere. Not a rocking bassinet. Do not confuse with the HALO Flex, which Consumer Reports flagged. halosleep.com
  • Skip: Graco Sense2Snooze (repeated malfunction reports), 4moms mamaRoo Sleep (not cry-responsive), Maxi-Cosi Iora (not smart at all).

The surprises, verified today

  1. FDA warning letter, June 15, 2026. Cited unauthorized XS and XL sleep sacks, mold and stains on refurbished units, and hospital-bundle marketing. No injuries determined. Your baby will be in S and M sacks, so the sack issue does not apply; the sanitation issue means inspect any rental or Pre-Loved unit on arrival and photograph it before first use. Consumer Reports
  2. The new price is $1,995 today, not the $1,695 in most articles. Labor Day (Sep 3–7) had it at $1,356; the next likely sale is Black Friday, which is too late for you.
  3. Buying and returning is expensive: $199 restocking plus an $89 return shipping fee, 30-day window from delivery.
  4. Rental fees are non-refundable once processing starts (initial rental fee and reconditioning fee), and return shipping is $59.50 + tax on top.
  5. Used SNOO from a stranger = free app only. Day-to-day works (rocking, sound, motion limiter), but weaning mode, level lock and sleep logs cost $19.99/mo. Official new, Pre-Loved and rental units include premium.
  6. Hard stop at 6 months or first push-up, whichever first. Big babies leave around 5 months. February is the plan, not a promise.
  7. One retailer (The Baby Cubby) shows the SNOO out of stock at the manufacturer. The official site shows in stock with a Sep 23 ship date, so order direct.
  8. Sacks are proprietary. Rental ships S and M only; you will buy an L (~$35–$40) around month 4.

Used-unit inspection checklist (do this before paying)

  • Plug in, press the button, run it through all 5 levels; listen for clicking or grinding (o-ring and motor wear).
  • Clip a sack in, press it on the bed, confirm it will not start without the sack clipped (safety interlock).
  • Connect the free app on your phone, confirm Wi-Fi pairing works and the unit is not still locked to the seller's account.
  • Check the mattress cover and sack seams for mold or stains; smell the bed.
  • Count the sacks (you want at least one M and one L) and ask for the leg risers if they have them.

What 256 sources say goes wrong (NotebookLM, ranked by how many sources report it)

Applies-to key: R = rental, P = Pre-Loved, U = used from a stranger, N = new. Source counts in parentheses.
  1. App paywall (18). Weaning mode, level lock, responsiveness tuning and sleep logs sit behind $19.99/mo since July 2024. Official R/P/N units include it; U does not. The free app still runs the bed day to day, including the motion limiter.
  2. O-ring breakdown and grinding motor (11). The rubber O-rings crumble to black dust and the sway goes jerky or stops. Age-driven, so it hits P and U hardest. Listen for it on the inspection.
  3. True rental cost (10). Reconditioning ~$99.50, return shipping $59.50+, a box fee (~$49.95) if you lost the box, and deposit deductions for stains. Priced into the table above except the box fee: keep the box. R.
  4. Crib transition (8). Babies get used to rocking plus swaddle and the 4–6 month move to a crib is rough. Start weaning mode around month 4. All.
  5. Safety and the FDA letter (7). XS/XL sacks unapproved, swaddle-slide reports. Use S/M only, clip the wings every time. All.
  6. Mold and stained units on arrival (6). R, P, U. Inspect and photograph before first use; Happiest Baby will swap a rental.
  7. Baby hates it (5). Some newborns fuss harder as the motion escalates. Rental or a local used unit limits the damage; a new purchase costs $288 to return.
  8. Glitchy app, random shutoffs (5). Laggy at 3 a.m., weaning settings not holding. All.
  9. No official repairs or spare parts (4). Happiest Baby will not sell O-rings or motors or service out-of-warranty units; DIY kits (~$23 on Marketplace) only. P after year one, U.
  10. Loud speaker, high minimum volume (4). All.
  11. Heavy, does not fold (3). Hard to move rooms or travel. All.
  12. Flat head, neck tightness, delayed rolling (3). Reported, not established; tummy time offsets. All.
