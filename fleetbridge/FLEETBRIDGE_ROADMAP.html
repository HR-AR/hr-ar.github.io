THE THESIS

"Software is the wedge; finance is the business."

FleetBridge = Toast for delivery fleets.

Start with fleet management software. Expand into embedded payments, insurance, and lending.

ACQUISITION TARGETS (Ranked)

  1. Nash — Supply-side complement. $15-50M.
  2. Bringg — Downmarket expansion. $10-30M.
  3. Onfleet — Technology + customer base. $10-25M.
  4. Shopify — Logistics vertical. $20-75M.
  5. Stripe — Embedded finance vertical. $25-100M.
  6. Amazon — Fleet management for DSP program. $50-150M.

KEY DECISIONS MADE

  1. Kill 1% commission — Stripe eats it. SaaS + hybrid instead.
  2. 4 revenue streams — SaaS + delivery fees + instant payout + insurance.
  3. "Shopify for delivery fleets" — new category, not Nash competitor.
  4. Checkr for background checks — 95% gig adoption, API-first.
  5. Don't fork Fleetbase — AGPL kills acquisition value.
  6. Insurance = moat — no competitor has embedded insurance.
  7. Start in DFW — one city, saturate before expanding.
  8. Fleet tool first, marketplace second — Onfleet model.
  9. Toast = north star — software wedge, finance business.
  10. React Native + Expo + Supabase — fastest to MVP.
  11. India team via Deel EOR — $6-10K/month for 2-3 devs.
  12. Payment via Venmo — resolved.
